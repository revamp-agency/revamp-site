# Content Master — Revamp

Every word on the site. Section by section, Italian + English.

**Copy principles:**
- Headlines do the heavy lifting. Body text is supporting, never redundant.
- Short. No paragraph should exceed 3 lines on desktop.
- Numbers are visual anchors — display them large.
- No jargon. A restaurant owner in Brescia must understand every word.
- "Tu" throughout Italian copy.
- Facts over promises. State what we do, not what we dream.
