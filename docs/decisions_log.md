## Positioning

- **Target audience:** Italian SMEs — lower/mid-tier business owners (restaurants, medical, legal, retail) who find €1,000+ websites too expensive
- **Geographic focus:** Italy only for launch
- **Industry focus:** General — no vertical specialization yet
- **Competitive edge:** High quality + very competitive price (student-driven low overhead, AI-native workflow) — **never stated explicitly on the site**
- **Framing on site:** Lead with outcome + speed + modernity. Let the price be the shock moment.
- **What we're NOT:** basic website builder, Fiverr-tier freelancer, big-4 consultant
- **Ventures separation:** ✅ **CONFIRMED** — Revamp site stays 100% focused on the agency. No mention of construction venture, ETA goals, holding company plans, or student/academic background. Revamp is its own clean brand.

## Pricing

- **Headline price:** **€200 one-time** (includes first year hosting + domain setup + everything needed to go live)
- **Year 2+ renewal:** €150/year (hosting, domain, minor fixes)
- **Founding offer:** €200 price is locked only for the **first 10 clients**. After that, entry price rises to €400.
- **Scarcity signal on site:** show "X posti rimasti su 10" counter (manually updated)
- **Pricing model:** Single fixed price, no tiers
- **Custom scope projects:** handled off-menu via call ("su preventivo")
- **Add-ons:** not displayed on site
- **Payment terms:** 50/50 (half upfront, half on delivery) — stated in FAQ
- **Maintenance plan:** €30/mese optional, offered post-delivery, **not displayed on public site**

## Process & operations

- **Delivery timeline shown on site:** "consegna rapida" — exact timeline set per-project on the discovery call
- **Revisions included:** 2 rounds, stated in FAQ
- **Additional revisions:** €50/round or hourly (not on site)
- **Post-launch support:** 30 days of free bug fixes included (stated in FAQ)
- **Hosting:** Vercel, under Revamp organization account, client gets viewer/collaborator seat
- **Domain ownership:** client owns the domain, registered in their name
- **Code ownership:** client owns the code, exportable any time — **stated on site as trust signal**
- **Client exit:** they can leave any time with their code — **stated on site as trust signal**
