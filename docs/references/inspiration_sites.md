# Inspiration Sites

Reference sites for Revamp's visual direction. 8 references gathered — notes on what to take from each.

---

## 1. Anton & Irene — https://antonandirene.com/
**What I want:** The dynamic background color shifts as you scroll — creates a sense of progression and keeps each section feeling distinct without hard borders. Some of the scroll-triggered animations are smooth and well-timed.
**What I don't want:** N/A
**Takeaway for Revamp:** Consider color-shifting backgrounds between homepage sections (e.g., hero → price shock → services each have a different tone that transitions on scroll).

---

## 2. Studio Freight — https://studiofreight.com/
**What I want:** The hover interaction on content blocks — when you hover on one block, the entire page transforms to surface more info about that element. This is exactly the interaction pattern we want for the services grid: user hovers on one of the 8 services, the whole viewport shifts to show details about that service.
**What I don't want:** N/A
**Takeaway for Revamp:** Apply this full-page hover-reveal pattern to the services grid on the homepage. Each of the 8 services becomes an interactive trigger, not just a card.

---

## 3. Locomotive — https://locomotive.ca/en
**What I want:** The hero block — an animated/looping video that plays as the first thing you see, then you scroll down into the rest of the page. We could produce a short branded animation or motion reel for Revamp's hero. These types of visual assets (videos, motion graphics) will be created separately and provided for integration into the code.
**What I don't want:** N/A
**Takeaway for Revamp:** Hero section = full-viewport looping video/animation. Content begins below the fold. We'll produce the video asset independently.

---

## 4. Zentry — https://zentry.com/
**What I want:** The overall futuristic aesthetic — bold oversized typography, layered scroll animations, immersive feel. The scale of the text and the confidence of the layout. Everything feels cinematic and intentional.
**What I don't want:** N/A
**Takeaway for Revamp:** Dial up the typography scale. Headlines should feel massive and cinematic. Scroll animations should be layered (parallax, reveals, scale shifts) not just simple fade-ins.

---

## 5. Bending Spoons — https://www.bendingspoons.com/
**What I want:** The animation quality throughout — smooth, polished, well-paced. Good reference for the overall motion design standard we're targeting.
**What I don't want:** N/A
**Takeaway for Revamp:** Use as a quality benchmark for animation polish. Every transition and reveal should feel this smooth.

---

## 6. Artefakt — https://artefakt.mov
**What I want:** The cursor-reactive text effect on the hero page — moving your cursor over the typography creates a visual distortion or interaction. Feels tactile and premium.
**What I don't want:** N/A
**Takeaway for Revamp:** Apply a cursor-reactive effect to the hero headline or tagline. Could be distortion, glow, color shift, or displacement on mouseover.

---

## 7. Mersi Architecture — https://www.mersi-architecture.com
**What I want:** The scroll-driven image transitions — images change smoothly as you scroll, almost like a cinematic sequence. The overall color palette and typography feel refined and editorial.
**What I don't want:** N/A
**Takeaway for Revamp:** Use smooth scroll-driven transitions between visual elements (not just text). Reference their typographic restraint — confident but not loud.

---

## 8. Auros Global — https://www.auros.global/
**What I want:** Two things: (1) the interactive particle/dot sphere that follows your cursor — creates a sense of depth and play. (2) The text color-change effect as you scroll past it. Want to push this further: not just on scroll, but also as a static ambient animation using tools like 21st.dev or similar pre-built interaction libraries.
**What I don't want:** N/A
**Takeaway for Revamp:** Explore an interactive 3D element (particle field, dot sphere, or similar) as a background or hero accent. Text color transitions should work both on scroll AND as idle ambient motion.

---

## Summary — key patterns across all references

| Pattern | Sources | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Scroll-driven color/background shifts | Anton & Irene, Auros | High |
| Full-page hover-reveal on services | Studio Freight | High — signature interaction |
| Hero video/animation loop | Locomotive | High — first impression |
| Oversized cinematic typography | Zentry | High |
| Cursor-reactive text effects | Artefakt | Medium-high |
| Interactive 3D particle element | Auros | Medium |
| Smooth scroll-driven image transitions | Mersi | Medium |
| Overall animation polish standard | Bending Spoons | Baseline quality bar |
