## What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function correctly, improve performance, and provide information to site owners.

## Cookies we use

### Technical cookies (necessary)

These cookies are essential for the site to function and cannot be disabled. They do not store personally identifiable information.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| cookie_consent | Stores your cookie preferences | 12 months |
| theme | Stores light/dark theme preference | 12 months |
| locale | Stores language preference (IT/EN) | 12 months |

### Analytics cookies

We use these cookies to understand how visitors interact with our site. All information is collected anonymously.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| _pk_* / plausible_* | Plausible Analytics | Anonymized traffic analysis | Session |

*Or, if using Google Analytics:*

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| _ga | Google Analytics | Distinguishes unique users | 24 months |
| _ga_* | Google Analytics | Maintains session state | 24 months |

**Note:** If we use Plausible Analytics, no third-party analytics cookies are used and no consent is required for analytics, as Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect personal data.

### Third-party cookies

Our site does not use profiling or advertising cookies.

## Managing cookies

On your first visit, a banner allows you to:
- **Accept all cookies**
- **Accept only necessary cookies**
- **Customize your preferences**

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking "Manage cookies" in the site footer.

You can also manage cookies directly from your browser:
- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
- Safari: Preferences → Privacy
- Firefox: Settings → Privacy and security
- Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
